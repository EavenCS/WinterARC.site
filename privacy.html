Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 August 2026 · Last updated: 26 August 2026 · Version 1.1

ARC is a local habit tracker. There is no account, no registration, no login, and no server operated by us. What you track stays on your iPhone.

The short version

  • Your name, focus areas, tasks, history, XP, streaks and blocking rules are stored only on your device, in a local database.
  • We never receive that data. We could not hand it over, sell it, or look at it even if we wanted to.
  • There is no tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising ID.
  • Step counts from Apple Health and Screen Time data never leave your device.
  • Data leaves your device only when you share a recap or a backup, when you buy or restore a subscription, and — automatically — when the app loads its fonts on first launch. Details in section 5.
  • Because we hold nothing, the only copy of your data is yours. Deleting the app deletes it for good.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the ARC app for iOS and this website. It does not cover Apple's services, the App Store, or any other app or service you may share your data with. Those are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. What ARC does

ARC is a habit tracker for a self-chosen “season” of 180 days. You pick focus areas, complete daily tasks, earn XP, and can block distracting apps. The app includes:

  • a choice of 15 focus areas, each with its own daily goals;
  • automatically generated daily tasks, plus tasks you create yourself;
  • XP, levels, ranks, streaks and milestones;
  • a timer for time-based goals;
  • blocking of selected apps in groups, with time budgets, session limits and blocking windows;
  • a waiting screen before a blocked app can be unlocked;
  • statistics and a monthly recap that can be shared as an image;
  • a daily local reminder at a time you choose;
  • export and import of your own data as a file;
  • three languages (German, English, Spanish) and a light and dark mode.

6. Permissions and why they exist

Every permission is optional. ARC remains usable without any of them; individual features simply stop working. You can withdraw each one at any time in the iOS Settings app, with effect for the future.

  • Screen Time / Family Controls — to block apps and to determine your total daily screen time. See section 8.
  • Apple Health (read-only) — the step count only, so step goals tick themselves off. ARC never writes to Health. See section 7.
  • Notifications — local reminders only. There is no push service and no server; notifications are scheduled by your device.
  • File access — only when you import a backup, and only for the single file you select.

7. Health data

Health data is a special category of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR and gets treated accordingly.

  • ARC reads only your step count from Apple Health, and only after you grant permission in the HealthKit dialog. That dialog is your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR.
  • The step count is used solely to complete step-based goals inside the app.
  • ARC writes nothing back to Apple Health.
  • Health data is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and is never used for advertising or marketing — this is also required by Apple's HealthKit rules.
  • You can revoke access at any time under Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices. Revoking it does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

8. Screen Time and app blocking

App blocking uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity frameworks. Apple's design deliberately keeps this data away from the app:

  • ARC never learns which apps you selected or how long you used them. Your selection is handed to the app only as an opaque, anonymous token that means nothing outside your device.
  • Evaluation happens inside a system extension that runs sandboxed on your device and returns nothing more than a total number of minutes.
  • The counters ARC does keep — attempts to open a blocked app, and times you resisted — are aggregate numbers stored locally, not a log of which app it was.

None of this is transmitted anywhere.

9. Subscriptions and payment

ARC is offered as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple is the seller and processes the payment. The app never sees your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no bank data.

Apple returns a purchase receipt so ARC can tell whether an active entitlement exists. Where RevenueCat GmbH/Inc. is used to validate that receipt, RevenueCat processes purchase and pseudonymous device identifiers as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR. It receives no season data, no health data and no screen time data. Their privacy notice: revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple's handling of the transaction is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

The commercial terms of the subscription are set out in the Terms of Service.

12. Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry.
  • “Restart season” irreversibly discards all progress data.
  • Deleting the app removes the local database and all settings with it.
  • A backup file you exported yourself is not affected by either — you have to delete that copy yourself, wherever you put it.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request, and thereafter only where statutory retention periods apply.

Because we hold no copy, deletion is genuinely final. There is no “restore from server” and no way for us to recover a season you have discarded. Export regularly if your history matters to you.

13. Children

ARC is intended for users aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly process any data of children. Users under 18 should only use ARC and only purchase a subscription with the consent of a parent or guardian.

14. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

A practical note. These rights apply to data a controller holds. We hold none of your app data — so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase. Your rights are exercised directly on your device: access it by opening the app, port it via Export, and erase it via Restart season or by deleting the app. For anything else — including data connected to a support email you sent us — write to schmalze6@gmail.com.
